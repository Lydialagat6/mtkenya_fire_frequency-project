
Research topic: Analysis of the relationship between maximum temperatures and fire frequency in Mt Kenya Forest from 2012 -2025. Case study Githiuri Forest Station
For this capstone 2 prompt, Average monthly temperatures for Githiuru forest was used

Research problem
Forest fires are increasing in frequency and intensity, with Mt. Kenya Forest - one of the country's five main water towers - being the most affected
To address this, this capstone project analyzes the relationship between average temperature and fire frequency in Mt. Kenya Forest from 2012-2025, to determine if warming is driving more fires.
A. Research questions
The main question: Is the increase in fire frequency caused by rising temperatures?
1.	How has fire frequency in Mt Kenya changed between 2012 and 2025?
2.	Is higher monthly temperatures associated with higher forest fire frequency?
3.	Do fires occur more frequently during dry-season months than wet-season months?
